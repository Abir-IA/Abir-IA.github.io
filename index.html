About Abir Ismaili Alaoui

I am an Assistant Professor at Université de Lorraine. I study how Digital Twins can stay faithful to the real processes they mirror, in hospitals and in factories.

Research

My research focuses on Digital Twin ecosystems as intelligent, data-driven frameworks for monitoring, decision-making, and optimization. The reliability of these systems depends on accurate, secure, and well-governed data, ensuring consistency and trust in real-time operations.

To make Digital Twins more adaptive and responsive, I integrate process mining to uncover inefficiencies and anomalies and ensure alignment with real-world processes, while Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) help them adapt to changing environments. My work bridges theoretical and applied perspectives, particularly in interdisciplinary domains such as healthcare and smart manufacturing.

  • Digital Twin
  • Multi-Agent Systems
  • Agent-based Simulation
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Data Governance
  • Business Process Management
  • Process Mining
  • Object-centric Process Mining

Current project

I am currently working on the JUNEAU project, funded by the French National Research Agency (ANR) (ANR-22-CE46-0010-01, October 2022 to October 2026), which aims to develop a Digital Twin for the CHU Saint-Étienne Emergency Department. The objective is to enable real-time visualization, predictive capabilities, and decision support to optimize patient flow and reduce emergency waiting times.
